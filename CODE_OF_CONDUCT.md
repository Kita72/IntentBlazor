# Community Code of Conduct

IntentBlazor welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, accept constructive feedback, credit others, and help newcomers participate. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-organized events. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or remove access when needed to protect the community. They will consider context, severity, and repeated behaviour.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact route listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Do not publish private details in an issue. Reports will be handled discreetly; information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

A maintainer involved in a concern should not decide its outcome alone. Request an uninvolved reviewer where available. If none is available, the project owner must identify an appropriate independent reviewer before resolving a contested report.
